MEGA is a course on working with agents. Lessons and Challenges live on
hq.mega.dev; this base keeps notes and progress, not copies of lesson pages.
The human's goal: learn how agents work.

- Dashboard: https://hq.mega.dev/
- Daily start: say "mega start"; the agent fetches today's lesson and writes a brief to the dashboard.
