## Options for product

1. **Hold the list while on-call is aiming.** While the pointer is over the list (or a button has focus), new incidents wait in a "N new incidents" bar at the top and enter when the pointer leaves or on-call clicks the bar. Rows never move under the pointer. Cost: R1 "appear live" becomes "announced live, shown on request" while aiming.
2. **Keep the live list, make Ack safe.** Rows keep moving; Ack is refused if the row moved in the last second or so, or Ack asks "Ack INC-7562?" before it sends. Cost: one more click or a short wait on every ack.
3. **Keep the plan as it is and add undo.** The acked mark names the incident and offers "undo" for a few seconds. Cost: the wrong ack still goes out first, and the API has no un-ack call today.
4. **Accept the risk.** Ship as planned. On-call will sometimes ack an incident they did not pick, and the incident they picked stays open with nobody on it.

I would take option 1, because it removes the wrong ack instead of repairing it, and no incident is hidden: the bar says how many are waiting.
